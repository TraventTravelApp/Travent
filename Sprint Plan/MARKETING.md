# Marketing — Emma's Track

Marketing isn't a phase tacked on at the end. It runs in parallel every single sprint, from week 1. The cost of starting a brand and audience late is much higher than the cost of working on it while engineering builds.

This doc is Emma's working space for the marketing track.

## Why now

We can't wait until the app is "ready" to start. Specifically:
- **Social handles** need to be reserved before someone else takes them
- **Domain** needs to be locked in
- **Audience building takes months** — content posted today pays off at launch (week 17)
- **Positioning and value prop** influence the product itself — Nikki and Jaliah's work is sharper when they know who we're building for
- **Beta testers** come from social, so we need a following before week 12

## North star

> _Chronicle is the travel app for [target persona] who want [outcome] without [friction the market currently has]._

**Sprint 1 deliverable:** fill in that sentence and publish to the team.

## Working principles

- **Quality > volume in content.** Two great posts a week beats ten mediocre ones.
- **Build in public.** Behind-the-scenes content is cheap to produce and performs well in the early-stage / indie-app space.
- **Travel content performs best on TikTok and Instagram Reels.** Optimize for those formats.
- **Respond to every comment + review.** Engagement begets engagement.
- **Measure what matters:** waitlist signups, app installs, signup → first-trip conversion. Vanity metrics (followers, likes) are noise.

## Budget framing

Indie-app marketing is mostly free + time, plus some paid experiments late. Rough envelope through W24:
- Domain + hosting + email tool: ~$30/month
- Landing page tool: ~$15/month
- Posting tool: ~$15/month
- Paid UA experiments (W23): $100-500 one-time
- Sponsored creator(s) (W23): $500-2000 one-time
- **Total estimated through launch:** ~$1500-4000 depending on paid spend
